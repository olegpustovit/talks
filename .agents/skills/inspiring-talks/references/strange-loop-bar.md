# Strange Loop bar

Strange Loop (2009–2023, St. Louis) is the quality bar for talks in
this repo, whether or not the target conference is Strange Loop.

## Guiding principles (from thestrangeloop.com/about.html)

- **No marketing.** Keynotes are never sold to sponsors. A talk that
  reads as a product ad is much less likely to be accepted than one
  that covers a new idea or technique.
- **Tech, not process.** Talks are in general code-heavy, not
  process-oriented (agile, testing, etc.). There are many fine
  conferences in the process area. This is not one of them.
- **Technology stew.** Academia, industry, and a touch of weirdness.
  Interesting stuff happens when you get people from different areas
  in the same room.

## What a selected talk actually does

From the CFP and program-selection notes:

- The **title** contains one or two nouns that convey the topic,
  language, or technique. Two-part snowclone titles ("The Hitchhiker's
  Guide to FOO", "FOO is the new BAR") are weaker if you delete one
  half and nothing is lost — delete it.
- The **abstract** is 3–5 sentences that answer "why should I attend,
  what will I learn?" It is not a teaser and not a blog post.
- **Reviewer info** lists the actual techniques, not vibes. If the
  abstract promises three techniques and the reviewer notes do not
  name them, the program committee assumes you cannot deliver.
- The program balances concept talks, deep technical talks, and
  fun/inspiring talks. "Inspiring" here still has an idea, not a
  pep talk.

## How we translate that into a deck

| Bar | In this repo |
|-----|----------------|
| Tech not process | Proof is code, a runtime, a failing experiment |
| No marketing | Tool named after the problem; honest cost slide |
| One thesis | `RESEARCH.md` states it; titles argue it |
| Code-heavy | `RevealCode` / `LiveCode` / a tiny teaching program |
| Weirdness allowed | A planted joke or a fake-out (Bernhardt) is fine if it serves the thesis |
| Abstract quality | `CFP.md` is 3–5 sentences with named takeaways |

If a draft would be rejected as "an ad for a product" or "process",
say that in feedback. Do not soften it into "maybe add more code".
